## What are ERGs and Communities at Meridian?

Meridian has twelve active communities organized into three types:

| Type | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Employee Resource Groups (ERGs)** | Belonging, inclusion, advocacy, peer support | Women in Tech, LGBTQ+ Alliance, Cultural Mosaic |
| **Communities of Practice** | Skill development, knowledge sharing, craft | Cloud Guild, Data Science Community, AI/GenAI Guild |
| **Interest Groups** | Social connection, shared hobbies | Outdoor Adventure, Foodies & Home Chefs, Investors & Markets |

Membership in any community is voluntary and opt-in. You can join as many as you want. Being active in even one community meaningfully accelerates how quickly you build your network at Meridian — especially in your first 90 days.

## Employee Resource Groups

### Women in Tech
**What it is:** Meridian's largest ERG. A community for women and allies in technology and consulting roles — focused on career development, visibility, and building a supportive network across all levels.

**What we do:**
- Monthly professional development sessions (technical skills, leadership, public speaking)
- Annual "Women in Consulting" summit — internal speakers + one external guest
- Informal mentorship pairing for women in their first two years at Meridian
- Ally programme — open to all, no experience required

**How to join:** `#women-in-tech` on Slack. First meeting of every month — invite in the company calendar. Co-chairs: **April Frost** (Senior Consultant, Data), **Sandra Aguilar** (Senior Consultant, App Dev).

---

### LGBTQ+ Alliance
**What it is:** a safe, visible community for LGBTQ+ employees and allies at Meridian. Focused on inclusion, advocacy, and creating a workplace where every person can bring their full self to work.

**What we do:**
- Monthly social sessions (casual, no agenda)
- Annual Pride Month programme (internal speakers, client event participation)
- Advocacy working group — contributes to Meridian's DEI policy development
- Connection to external networks (Out in Tech, Lesbians Who Tech)

**How to join:** `#lgbtq-alliance` on Slack. Sessions are the third Thursday of every month.

---

### Cultural Mosaic
**What it is:** a community celebrating Meridian's cultural diversity — connecting people across national backgrounds, languages, and traditions.

**What we do:**
- Quarterly cultural celebration events (food, music, storytelling)
- Language exchange programme
- Cross-cultural communication workshops for client-facing staff
- "Mosaic conversations" — informal discussions on culture, identity, and belonging

**How to join:** `#cultural-mosaic` on Slack. Events listed in the company calendar.

---

### Early Career Network
**What it is:** the community for Analysts and Consultants in their first three years at Meridian. Focused on accelerating your ramp, building cross-practice relationships, and navigating the jump from university or early career to consulting.

**What we do:**
- Monthly "How Meridian Works" sessions — covering everything from how engagements are sold to how performance reviews work
- Peer mentorship circles — small groups of 4–6 people at similar levels, same cohort start
- Senior mentor matching — connect with a Manager or above outside your practice
- Annual Early Career retreat (1 day, in-person when possible)

**How to join:** `#early-career` on Slack. Open to all Analysts and Consultants regardless of start date. Your onboarding checklist includes an invitation to your first session. Co-chairs: **Nathan Freeman** (Consultant, Strategy), **Debra Christian** (Consultant, Data).

## Communities of Practice

### Cloud Guild
**What it is:** the cross-practice home for cloud practitioners at Meridian — regardless of whether you sit in Cloud, Data, App Dev, or Cyber. Focused on technical depth, AWS/Azure/GCP updates, and sharing patterns across practices.

**Cadence:** bi-weekly guild call (alternating demo and discussion format). `#cloud-guild` on Slack.
**Leadership:** **Tracy House** (Manager, Cloud) chairs the guild.

---

### Data Science Community
**What it is:** the community for data engineers, analysts, data scientists, and ML practitioners. The place where the Data practice's institutional knowledge actually lives — retrospectives, tool evaluations, pattern libraries.

**Cadence:** monthly all-community session + weekly `#data-community` Slack activity. Office hours every Thursday 12–1pm EST (open question format).
**Go-to people:** **Courtney Gonzalez** (Databricks, Unity Catalog), **Ethan Adams** (data engineering architecture), **Angie Henderson** (practice strategy).

---

### AI/GenAI Guild
**What it is:** Meridian's fastest-growing community — practitioners exploring generative AI, LLMs, Amazon Bedrock, and agentic workflows. Cross-practice (you don't need to be in Data or App Dev).

**Cadence:** bi-weekly. Sessions alternate between external paper reviews and internal demo showcases. `#ai-genai-guild` on Slack.

---

### Security Community
**What it is:** the practitioner community for the Cyber practice and security-adjacent roles. Covers threat intelligence, red team techniques, GRC, and cloud security.

**Cadence:** monthly. `#security-community` on Slack.

## How to get the most out of communities

**Show up before you need anything.** The people who build the best networks at Meridian are in communities from week one — not when they need a referral or a favour. Show up to give, not to take.

**ERGs are not just for the underrepresented majority of the group.** Women in Tech has strong male allies. The LGBTQ+ Alliance has straight members. Allies move culture as much as the people the group is named for.

**Post in the Slack channels.** The communities that matter most are the ones where people are actually talking. A question posted in `#data-community` will get answered by a Senior Manager within a few hours. That's the network.

*Questions about any community? Reach out to People & Culture or the community co-chairs listed above.*
