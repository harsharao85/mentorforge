# Navigating Your First 90 Days at Northstar Consulting

**For:** All new hires, across all practices and levels

---

## Welcome

Your first 90 days will be the fastest, most disorienting, and most formative period of your time at Northstar. Everyone who is here now has been exactly where you are.

---

## Week 1: Get Oriented

Resist the urge to produce something impressive immediately. The context you gather in Week 1 saves you weeks of rework later.

**Priority actions:**
1. Complete your Day 1 admin tasks — your onboarding journey checklist has everything. If something is stuck, your buddy is your first call.
2. Meet your manager — have an honest conversation about what success looks like in your first 30 days.
3. Meet your buddy — ask the questions you're embarrassed to ask your manager.
4. Shadow, don't just read — sit in on your team's standups before taking on deliverables.

---

## Days 30–60: Build Your Network

**What to do:**
- Attend at least one ERG meeting or community of practice session. The ERGs and guilds are how you meet people outside your immediate team.
- Complete your **30-day check-in** with your manager.

**Thin-network warning:** MentorForge tracks whether new hires are building connections. If the system flags you as thin-networked at Day 30, a manager nudge is automatically queued — a backstop for busy managers who might otherwise miss a new hire slipping through.

Aisha Rahman is in her first 30 days in the Strategy practice and is a member of Women in Tech and the Newcomers ERG. Cohort connections are often the most durable friendships at Northstar.

---

## Day 60–90: Contribute

By Day 60 you are expected to be contributing independently. The 90-day mark looks like this when it goes well:
- You know who to call for the five most common problems in your role
- You have attended at least one ERG or community session
- Your manager can describe your contributions in concrete terms

The **90-day review** is a conversation, not a performance appraisal. Come prepared to talk about what you learned and what you want next.

---

## ERGs and Communities — Join One

- **Women in Tech** — monthly events, mentoring circles, external speaker series
- **Newcomers** — for people in their first year; bi-monthly socials, peer support
- **Cloud Guild, Data Science Community, AI/GenAI Guild** — technical deep dives
- **Outdoor Adventure, Investors & Markets, Foodies & Home Chefs** — interest groups

Find and join communities in MentorForge → Your Journey → Community.

---

## Three Things That Matter More Than You Think

1. **Show up to the cohort social.** It feels optional. It isn't.
2. **Tell your manager about blockers early.**
3. **Ask your buddy the awkward questions.** That is what they are there for.
