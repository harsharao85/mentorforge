# Newcomers — Northstar Consulting ERG

**Type:** Employee Resource Group (ERG)
**Membership:** Open to all employees in their first 12 months
**Meets:** Bi-monthly socials + quarterly panel events

---

## Who We Are

Newcomers is the ERG for everyone who is still figuring out how Northstar works. It exists because the first year at a consulting firm is genuinely hard, and peer support from people going through the same thing is more useful than most onboarding programs.

You do not have to be shy, introverted, or struggling to benefit from Newcomers. Many of our most active members are confident people who simply found that having a peer community accelerated their integration into the firm.

---

## What We Do

**Bi-monthly socials:** Low-key in-person or virtual events where new hires across all practices and locations get together. No agenda. Just people.

**First-90-days panel:** Each quarter, a panel of people who joined in the past year shares their honest experience: what was hard, what helped, what they wish they had known. Hosted by Newcomers, open to all.

**Buddy pairing (ERG-led):** We run an informal peer buddy system within the ERG — separate from the firm's formal buddy program. Your ERG buddy is a peer in their first 18 months; your firm buddy is more senior. Both are useful.

**Slack community:** `#newcomers` is an active channel for questions you would feel awkward asking anywhere else.

---

## Current Members (May 2026 Cohort)

The May cohort includes Priya Nair (Data, Vancouver), Marcus Lee (Cloud, Toronto), Aisha Rahman (Strategy, London), and ~27 others from the current onboarding cohort. We are a geographically distributed group and run remote-first events.

## How to Join

Open MentorForge → Your Journey → Community → Newcomers → Join. Or join `#newcomers` in Slack directly.

If you are a new hire reading this during onboarding: **show up to the first social you can.** The people you meet there will be the people you still know in five years.
