# Women in Tech — Northstar Consulting ERG

**Type:** Employee Resource Group (ERG)
**Membership:** 180+ members across all practices and seniority levels
**Leads:** Rotating quarterly; current lead team based in Vancouver and London

---

## Who We Are

Women in Tech is Northstar's largest ERG. We exist to support and advance women and gender-diverse colleagues across every practice, level, and location in the firm. Our members range from first-year Analysts to Partners.

We are not a coffee-and-networking group. We run substantive programming: mentoring circles, external speaker series, technical skill-sharing sessions, and a leadership pipeline track for Senior Consultants and Managers who want to move into leadership roles.

---

## What We Do

**Monthly events:** A mix of technical workshops, career conversations, and social events. Recent topics have included "Negotiation skills for consulting careers," "Being the only woman in the room," and "AI in practice: opportunities and risks for women in tech."

**Mentoring circles:** Small groups of 4–6 members, matched by career stage. Applications open each quarter. Current participants include Aisha Rahman (Strategy practice, London) and Priya Nair (Data practice, Vancouver), among others in the May cohort.

**External speaker series:** Two per year. Past speakers have included founders, CTOs, and partners from across the industry.

**ERG leadership track:** If you are a Senior Consultant or Manager interested in developing your leadership skills, the ERG leadership program offers structured opportunities to lead initiatives, manage a team of volunteers, and present to the full ERG.

---

## How to Join

Every new hire is automatically eligible for Women in Tech membership. To join:
1. Open MentorForge → Your Journey → Community → Women in Tech → Join
2. Introduce yourself in the `#womenintech` Slack channel
3. You will receive an invite to the next monthly event within 48 hours

**New hires in their first 30 days:** You are actively welcomed and do not need a connection or introduction. Just show up.
