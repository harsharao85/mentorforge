# Data Science Community — Northstar Consulting Community of Practice

**Type:** Community of Practice
**Membership:** ~90 members across Data and ML & AI teams
**Meets:** Monthly — first Tuesday, 12pm PST

---

## Who We Are

The Data Science Community is the cross-practice learning space for data, analytics, ML, and AI practitioners. We are practice-agnostic — Data Engineering, Analytics & BI, ML & AI, Strategy, and Cloud practice members all participate.

---

## What We Cover

Sessions rotate between hands-on technical content and practitioner discussions. Recent topics:
- "Databricks Delta Live Tables in production — lessons from Atlas-Health" — Ben Okonkwo
- "dbt at scale: the things the docs don't tell you" — Tom Rivera
- "GraphRAG in enterprise: what we've learned at Northstar" — Data practice
- "Responsible AI in client engagements" — ML & AI team
- "Feature Store vs. Unity Catalog Feature Engineering: when to use what"

---

## The dbt Working Group

Tom Rivera runs a dbt working group that meets every other Wednesday. It is separate from the monthly session and focuses on practical dbt patterns, testing strategies, and macro development. Reach out to Tom to join.

If you are a new hire in the Data practice — especially Priya Nair, who is joining the Atlas-Health data engineering team — the dbt working group is highly recommended.
