# App Dev Practice Standards Policy

**Owner:** App Dev Practice, Northstar Consulting
**Applicability:** All staff on client engagements and similar engagements

## Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for app dev work at Northstar Consulting. All staff are expected to adhere to these guidelines on client engagements.

## Requirements

1. All deliverables must follow the App Dev practice standards.
2. Client data handling must comply with the relevant regulatory framework.
3. Documentation must be complete before engagement closeout.

Questions → App Dev Practice Lead or your engagement manager.
