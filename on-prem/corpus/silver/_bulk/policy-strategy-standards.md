# Strategy Practice Standards Policy

**Owner:** Strategy Practice, Northstar Consulting
**Applicability:** All staff on client engagements and similar engagements

## Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for strategy work at Northstar Consulting. All staff are expected to adhere to these guidelines on client engagements.

## Requirements

1. All deliverables must follow the Strategy practice standards.
2. Client data handling must comply with the relevant regulatory framework.
3. Documentation must be complete before engagement closeout.

Questions → Strategy Practice Lead or your engagement manager.
